FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our drinking water and delivery process.

What is the water purification process of AquaPure?
AquaPure employs an advanced 8-stage purification cycle comprising sediment filtration, activated carbon absorption, reverse osmosis (RO), ultraviolet (UV) sterilization, ozonization, and copper + mineral balancing to ensure crisp taste and maximum safety.
How fast can I get delivery in Dattawadi, Nagpur?
We deliver within 2 to 4 hours of placing an instant order. For subscription customers, delivery happens automatically in your selected morning (7 AM - 10 AM) or evening time slot.
Do I have to pay any security deposit for 20L cans?
No! On monthly subscription plans, we provide cans with zero deposit hassle. You simply return the empty can upon delivery of the fresh sanitized refill can.
Can I pause or reschedule my subscription if I am travelling?
Yes, absolutely. Just WhatsApp us on +91 9201767987 at least 12 hours prior, and we will pause your deliveries. Your plan validity will automatically extend by the paused days.
